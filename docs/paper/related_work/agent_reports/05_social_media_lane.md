## Closest papers (ranked by threat/relevance)

1. **BotTrans: Multi-Source Graph Domain Adaptation for Social Bot Detection** — ECML-PKDD 2025 (oral) — arXiv:2506.13795 — Trains on multiple *labeled* bot-detection source graphs and transfers to an unlabeled target graph via cross-source-domain topology. RELATION: **adjacent-but-open, forces softening** — it *is* multi-graph→new-graph transfer on social networks, but supervised domain adaptation for one task, not SSL pretraining with frozen few-shot probes across diverse tasks. Our "nobody has shown additive gains from pretraining on multiple social graphs" is falsified in its unqualified form.
2. **MiNT: Multi-Network Training for Transfer Learning on Temporal Graphs** — NeurIPS 2025 Datasets & Benchmarks — arXiv:2406.10426 — Pretrains one model on up to 64 temporal networks, zero-shot transfers to 20 unseen ones; more pretraining networks → better transfer. RELATION: **scoops the paradigm, not the domain** — the "multi-network pretraining scaling" story exists, but on token-transaction networks, not social media; must-cite and differentiate.
3. **SoMeR: Multi-View Social Media User Representation Learning** — ICWSM 2026 (**published June 2026**, arXiv May 2024) — arXiv:2405.05275 — Transformer user embeddings from posts/profile/network with link-prediction + contrastive SSL; applied to IO-driver detection, polarization, Reddit hate. RELATION: **adjacent-but-open** — SSL user representations for social media, but a framework instantiated per dataset; no single backbone pretrained across graphs, no transfer matrix.
4. **Social-LLM** (Jiang & Ferrara) — arXiv:2401.00893, journal version in *Sci* 7(4):138, 2025 — LLM text embeddings + local network interactions for scalable inductive user embeddings; evaluated on 7 Twitter datasets incl. **account suspension** and **partisanship**. RELATION: **must-cite baseline** — shares two of our exact tasks and the bio-embedding+graph recipe, but trains per-corpus, no cross-corpus pretraining/transfer.
5. **TwHIN** — KDD 2022 — arXiv:2202.05387 — Twitter's production heterogeneous-network embeddings (10^9 nodes, 10^11 edges), pretrained then reused for ads, follow-rec, offensive-content. RELATION: **falsifies an over-broad claim** — a pretrained social-graph backbone *did* exist inside Twitter; it is single-platform, single-graph, shallow KG-embedding (not inductive GNN-SSL), no cross-event transfer. Phrase our claim to survive this.
6. **SATAR** — CIKM 2021 — arXiv:2106.13089 — Self-supervised Twitter user representation (follower-count pretext), pretrain-then-finetune for bot detection. RELATION: **must-cite precedent** for SSL user pretraining; single corpus, fine-tuned not frozen.
7. **AnyGraph / OpenGraph / SAMGPT / GCOPE lane** — arXiv:2408.10700, 2403.01121, 2502.05424 — generic multi-domain GFMs pretrained on dozens of benchmark graphs (e-commerce, citation, trust, email...). RELATION: **must-cite** — they claim cross-domain universality but include no event-scale social-media graphs and no social tasks (bot/suspension/ideology).
8. **TwiBot-22** — NeurIPS 2022 D&B — arXiv:2206.04564 — Besides the benchmark, it runs the best-known bot-detector **generalization study across datasets** (graph methods generalize best). RELATION: must-cite baseline for cross-corpus evaluation framing.
9. **BotArtist** — arXiv:2306.00037 (Springer volume 2025) — feature-based classifier trained/evaluated across **9 bot datasets**, avg F1 83.19, beats 35 methods on generalization. RELATION: must-cite — closest existing "transfer matrix over social corpora," but profile-features ML, no graphs, no pretraining.
10. **Unmasking the Web of Deceit** (Luceri et al.) — WWW 2024 — SSRN 4614245 / related arXiv:2409.15402 — cross-campaign IO-driver detection over 49M tweets, 6 countries; fused similarity networks generalize across campaigns. RELATION: must-cite for cross-event supervised transfer in CSS.
11. **Russo-Ukrainian War: Prediction and explanation of Twitter suspension** — ASONAM 2023 — arXiv:2306.03502 — 9.8M users/107.7M tweets, ML+SHAP suspension prediction. RELATION: must-cite baseline (our suspension task's direct prior; feature-based, single corpus — likely same data lineage as ours, check overlap).
12. **Retweet-BERT** — ICWSM 2023 — arXiv:2207.08459 — political-leaning detection from profile text + retweet diffusion. RELATION: must-cite baseline for the ideology task (with Barberá 2015).

## Answers to the six questions

**1. Does a social-network GFM exist?** No public model is pretrained across multiple social networks with SSL and reused frozen. What exists: (a) single-platform industrial backbones — TwHIN (2202.05387), LiGNN (2402.11139), PinnerFormer/PinSage, 360Brew (2501.16450), USER-LLM (2402.13598) — all one platform, recsys-oriented, mostly supervised/KG objectives; (b) per-corpus academic frameworks — Social-LLM (2401.00893), SoMeR (2405.05275); (c) generic GFMs (AnyGraph 2408.10700; OpenGraph 2403.01121; GraphAny 2405.20445; universal-GFM arXiv:2604.06391, **Apr 2026**, bio-focused) whose corpora contain at most small generic "social" benchmark graphs, never event-scale Twitter interaction graphs or social-media tasks. The GFM survey (2505.15116) lists social networks only as a generic motivating domain — no dedicated social GFM cited.

**2. Cross-event/cross-corpus transfer.** Rumor/misinfo: cross-event few-shot metric learning (Neurocomputing 2023), zero-shot propagation-prompt (AAAI 2023), PaPa (2025), test-time adaptation (EMNLP 2025) — all cascade-level, one task. Coordination: Luceri et al. WWW 2024 cross-campaign IO detection; cross-platform IO (2409.15402); community-model generalization critique (2404.01822). Bots: TwiBot-22 generalization study; LOBO (ACSAC 2018); Yang et al. 2020; BotArtist 9-dataset tables; BotTrans multi-source DA (2506.13795). **Nobody has published an N×N SSL-pretraining transfer matrix over social-media graphs** — the closest are supervised train-on-A-test-on-B tables for a single task.

**3. TwiBot-20 SOTA & protocol.** Supervised full-label SOTA sits at ~87–90 Acc / 86–88 F1: BotRGCN F1≈0.87; RGT 86.5/87.9; SeBot (KDD 2024) tops both TwiBot-20 and MGTAB; LMBot (WSDM 2024) +0.97% Acc; BotDMM 88.3/86.3; one IEEE 2024 ResNet paper claims 94.1% Acc (non-standard setup — treat as outlier). LLM detectors (2402.00371) beat baselines only after instruction tuning; zero-shot LLMs struggle. SSL appears only as SATAR (single-corpus pretrain→finetune) and BotSSCL (contrastive, tabular features, 2025). **Nobody uses cross-corpus pretraining.** The community protocol is full supervision on the fixed 7:2:1 split reporting Acc/F1; our frozen few-shot ~0.8-AUC numbers are a different protocol and *will* be read as non-comparable unless we (i) frame as label-efficiency/frozen-transfer, (ii) print the supervised skyline in the same table, and (iii) add a same-protocol single-graph baseline.

**4. Suspension & ideology priors.** Suspension: arXiv:2306.03502 (ASONAM 2023, Ukraine-war corpus — likely the *same* data lineage as ours); Social-LLM includes suspension among its 7 tasks; earlier deleted/suspended-account prediction work used language+image+network. Ideology: Barberá 2015 ideal points; Retweet-BERT (ICWSM 2023); unsupervised fine-grained leaning (2202.12382); DoubleH bipartite GNN (2301.08774); a 2025 line does purely network-structural ideology classification. All single-corpus, mostly full-supervision; none from a frozen multi-graph pretrained backbone.

## Verdict

**Keep the claim, but harden the wording.** Safe version: "No graph foundation model pretrained across multiple social networks exists: industrial systems (TwHIN, LiGNN) pretrain on a single platform for recommendation; academic user-representation frameworks (Social-LLM, SoMeR) train per corpus; generic GFMs (AnyGraph, OpenGraph) contain no event-scale social graphs or social-media tasks." Do **not** write "nobody has shown gains from multiple social graphs" unqualified — BotTrans shows supervised multi-source gains for bot detection; write "no one has demonstrated **additive gains from self-supervised pretraining** across multiple social graphs on **diverse** downstream tasks," and cite MiNT as the paradigm's proof-of-concept outside social media.

**Baselines reviewers will demand:** supervised skylines on TwiBot-20 (BotRGCN/RGT/SeBot/LMBot numbers in-table), SATAR and BotSSCL (SSL precedents), Social-LLM-style bio-embedding+shallow-graph baseline, single-graph SSL (pretrain on target only — we have), a generic GFM zero/few-shot baseline (AnyGraph or PRODIGY-citation-pretrained), BotArtist/TwiBot-22-style cross-dataset supervised transfer, Retweet-BERT or label propagation for ideology, and the 2306.03502 feature model for suspension.

**Venue fit:** the method+multi-graph-benchmark framing fits **WSDM 2027 (Aug 24, 2026 deadline)** or **WWW 2027** best — both publish the closest graph/social hybrids; **ICWSM 2027 (Jan 15, 2027 round)** if reframed toward CSS insight (transfer structure between events), and **NeurIPS 2026/2027 D&B** if the 8-graph corpus + transfer matrix is released as a benchmark (TwiBot-22 and MiNT precedents). Key post-Jan-2026 items flagged: GFM benchmark (Feb 2026), Mochi (Apr 2026), universal GFM (Apr 2026), feature-bridging GFM (May 2026), TRACE-Bot (Apr 2026), SoMeR's ICWSM publication (Jun 2026), WWW 2026 narrative paper, BOT-InFUSED2 (2026).

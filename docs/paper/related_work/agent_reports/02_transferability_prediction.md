# Lit sweep 2/5 — Transferability estimation, graph domain adaptation, source selection

*Deep literature agent report, 2026-07-25. Post-Jan-2026 arXiv swept explicitly. Verbatim.*

## Closest papers (ranked by threat/relevance)

1. **GNNMTE: GNN Model Transferability Estimation via Decomposition-Augmented Discriminant Analysis** — WWW 2026 (proceedings 10.1145/3774904.3792172; **post-Jan-2026, conference ~April 2026**) — no arXiv ID found; ACM full text is paywalled (403), so characterization is from abstract/snippets. First dedicated transferability-estimation method for GNNs: scores pre-trained models from a model hub for a target graph without fine-tuning; explicitly states LogME/LEEP-style MTE "mainly focus on image or text" and graphs are "underexplored". RELATION: **must-cite baseline + partial scoop pressure** — it claims the "transferability estimation for graphs" flag, but it does *model* selection given a target, not pre-hoc *source-corpus* selection for SSL pretraining, and (from available text) is embedding/discriminant-based, not a feature-divergence account.
2. **W2PGNN: When to Pre-Train GNNs? From Data Generation Perspective!** — KDD 2023 — arXiv 2303.16458 — fits pretraining graphs into graphon bases; convex hull of bases defines which downstream data can benefit; applications include feasibility quantification and *pre-training data selection*. RELATION: **must-cite baseline** — the canonical pre-hoc feasibility/source-selection predictor, and it is topology-generative (graphon), i.e., exactly the structural competitor our feature-divergence predictor should beat.
3. **GSTBench: Benchmark Study on Transferability of Graph SSL** — arXiv 2509.06975 (Sept 2025) — pretrains 5 SSL methods (GraphMAE, VGAE, DGI, GRACE, LP) on ogbn-papers100M, transfers to 8 targets; most SSL transfers poorly, GraphMAE best; hypothesizes generative success comes from "reconstructing the rich LLM features" (feature-semantics mechanism). Reports MMD/homophily descriptively, **no predictor, single source, no source selection**. RELATION: **adjacent-but-open + must-cite** — convergent with our mechanism finding (SSL rides on text-feature content) but leaves the predictor/selection lane empty.
4. **EGI: Transfer Learning of GNNs with Ego-graph Information Maximization** — NeurIPS 2021 — arXiv 2009.05204 — transferability analyzed via "the difference between the local graph Laplacians of source and target graphs", valid "when node features are structure-relevant"; validated with controlled *synthetic structural* experiments. RELATION: **must-cite baseline** — the standard structural transferability measure; its structure-relevant-features assumption is precisely what our Twitter-bio-embedding setting violates.
5. **Tree Mover's Distance** — NeurIPS 2022 — arXiv 2210.01906 — hierarchical-OT pseudometric over node attributes + computation trees; correlates with GNN performance drop under distribution shift. RELATION: **must-cite baseline / methodological anchor** — the strongest existing attributed-graph distance; note it entangles features and structure, whereas we decompose them.
6. **Better with Less (APT): Data-Active Graph Pre-Training** — NeurIPS 2023 — arXiv 2311.01038 — selects fewer pretraining graphs via predictive uncertainty + structural properties (entropy, density, degree stats); "better with less" beats pretraining on everything. RELATION: **must-cite baseline** for pretraining-data selection; selection criteria are structure-property-based, target-agnostic — no feature-divergence, no target-conditioned donor choice.
7. **Subgraph Pooling: Tackling Negative Transfer on Graphs** — arXiv 2402.08907 — claims *structural* differences amplify node-embedding dissimilarity and drive negative transfer even between semantically similar graphs; evidence correlational + theory, no controlled manipulation. RELATION: **contradicts (partially)** — a structure-first account of cross-graph transfer failure that our feature-dominance result (and interventions) would directly counter.
8. **Towards Graph Foundation Models: A Transferability Perspective (survey)** — arXiv 2503.09363 (Mar 2025) — verified: taxonomy only; Section 7 states "The lack of standardized benchmarking for GFM transferability hinders progress… helping identify key factors that influence transferability"; treats topology/feature disparities jointly ("naive dataset aggregation may lead to negative transfer") and offers **no predictive metric and no source-selection guidance**. RELATION: **must-cite gap statement** — slightly weaker than "no validated metric exists" verbatim, but it confirms the field hasn't even identified the key factors.
9. **Pair-Align** (ICML 2024, arXiv 2403.01092) and **StruRW** (ICML 2023, arXiv 2306.03221) — GDA via edge reweighting for conditional structure shift (+ label-shift weights in Pair-Align). RELATION: must-cite baselines — structure-shift-centric GDA; assume a fixed source, no source selection.
10. **GDABench: Revisiting, Benchmarking and Understanding Unsupervised GDA** — arXiv 2407.11052 — 16 algorithms, 74 tasks; concludes structural shifts are the critical difficulty; simple GNN variants with the right aggregation beat SOTA UGDA. RELATION: adjacent-but-open; another structure-first narrative to engage.
11. **Graphon transferability theory** — Ruiz et al. (arXiv 2006.03548, 2008.01767), Levie et al. (1907.12972), Maskey et al. extended graphon (2109.10096), Transferability Properties (2112.04629) — bounds on output differences for graphs from the same graphon; purely structural, feature distribution held fixed. RELATION: methodological anchor (theory says structure; our data say features).
12. **WL meets Gromov-Wasserstein** — ICML 2022 — arXiv 2202.02495 (+ 2302.00713) — WL distance as poly-time GW lower bound; MPNNs Lipschitz w.r.t. it. RELATION: methodological anchor for structure-distance baselines (with FGW, Vayer et al. 2019, as the attributed variant).
13. **Graph Transfer Learning via Shared Latent Geometry** — arXiv 2606.00716 (**May 30, 2026 — post-Jan-2026**) — Wasserstein proximity between latent laws gives transfer conditions + zero-shot error bound with a finite-sample certification protocol; application: power systems, topology-varying instances. RELATION: adjacent theory anchor — closest 2026 "Wasserstein divergence predicts transfer" formalism, but latent-geometry, single domain, no source selection.
14. **GraphAlign** (arXiv 2406.02953) and **One Model for One Graph** (2412.00315) — multi-graph pretraining via feature alignment / per-graph expert banks with gating. RELATION: adjacent — community handles feature heterogeneity or routes among sources at inference, but with no pre-hoc predictor.
15. **Do Neural Scaling Laws Exist on Graph SSL?** — arXiv 2408.11243 — data scaling largely fails for graph SSL. RELATION: motivational must-cite — if more data doesn't help, *which* data becomes the question.

## Answers to the five questions

**1. Transferability estimation for graph pretraining.** EGI (2009.05204) is the only classic validated predictor: structural (local ego-graph Laplacian difference), assuming structure-relevant features. TMD (2210.01906) correlates an attribute+tree OT distance with shift-induced degradation. Graphon (2006.03548 line) and WL/GW (2202.02495) give structural bounds, never deployed as practical cross-corpus predictors. The GFM survey (2503.09363) confirms the gap: no standardized benchmarking, key factors influencing transferability unidentified, no metric proposed. 2025–26 additions: GNNMTE (WWW 2026) — model-hub selection via discriminant analysis, not source selection; GSTBench (2509.06975) — benchmark, explicitly no predictor. **No paper found that uses raw feature-space divergence as the validated predictor of cross-graph SSL transfer.** The closest spiritual relative is within-graph: Shift-Robust GNN / test-time-adaptation work (e.g., GTrans, 2210.03561) shows CMD between representations anticorrelates with accuracy under biased sampling — within one graph, representation-level, not cross-graph pretraining.

**2. Graph domain adaptation.** UDAGCN (WWW 2020): adversarial (gradient-reversal) alignment of node *representations*. StruRW (2306.03221) / Pair-Align (2403.01092): conditional-structure-shift edge reweighting (+ label shift). SpecReg (ICLR 2023): OT-derived bound → Lipschitz/spectral regularization (spectral-structural). A2GNN (2402.05660): propagation-layer asymmetry (structural). The field's stated emphasis (StruRW, GDABench 2407.11052, Subgraph Pooling 2402.08907) is that **structure shift is the hard part** — none demonstrates feature-divergence dominance, and **none does source selection**; all assume the source-target pair is given. This is a genuine framing collision our matrix + interventions can win.

**3. LogME/LEEP/H-score on graphs.** Essentially unported before 2026. GNNMTE's own framing confirms MTE literature is CV/NLP-bound and graphs "underexplored". No validation of LogME/LEEP/H-score for graph SSL checkpoints or source corpora was found; the awesome-model-transferability-estimation list surfaces no graph-SSL entries. Applying them as baselines (on frozen encoder embeddings) would itself be a novel comparison.

**4. Interventional evidence.** All cross-graph predictor evidence found is correlational. The only controlled manipulations are EGI's synthetic *structural* experiments and benchmark-constructed shift splits (GOOD-style); CSBM work controls feature/structure informativeness for architecture theory, not cross-graph transfer prediction. **No dose-response manipulation of feature-space divergence (rotation/interpolation) on real graphs found** — the interventional causal claim appears to be ours to make.

**5. Scoop check (2025–2026).** GNNMTE (WWW 2026) is the nearest, but is model selection. Others checked and clear: MUG (2602.22645, Feb 2026, heterogeneous pretraining), GraphBFF billion-scale GFM (2602.04768, Feb 2026 — scaling only, no data selection), DisRFM (2602.00656, Feb 2026, GDA), architecture-generalization (2603.22984, Mar 2026), Riemannian graph gluing (2603.00618, Mar 2026), S2Aligner (2605.18579, May 2026, TAG pretraining), shared-latent-geometry theory (2606.00716, May 2026). UniAug (2406.01899, NeurIPS'25) scales structure augmentation; One-Model-One-Graph (2412.00315) sidesteps selection with expert gating. Nobody validates "which pretraining graph should I use" via feature divergence.

## Verdict

**The lane is open as of July 2026.** No paper validates a feature-divergence predictor of cross-graph SSL transfer, none separates feature- from structure-divergence with interventions, and pre-hoc source selection exists only in structural/graphon (W2PGNN) or target-agnostic (APT) form. The GDA mainstream actively argues structure-shift-first, which makes a feature-dominance result contrarian rather than redundant. Two pressure points: GNNMTE plants the "graph transferability estimation" flag (cite and differentiate: model-selection vs source-selection; discriminant score vs interpretable divergence; no mechanism), and GSTBench already articulates the "SSL rides on LLM features" hypothesis — cite it as convergent motivation, not competition.

**Baselines we must compare against to be credible:** (1) EGI's structural ego-graph/Laplacian divergence; (2) W2PGNN's graphon-based feasibility score; (3) TMD; (4) FGW and/or WL-distance (attributed + structural OT distances); (5) LogME (+ LEEP/H-score) on frozen-encoder embeddings; (6) GNNMTE if reproducible from the WWW 2026 paper; plus cheap descriptors (degree/homophily-statistic deltas, spectral distances) as the "topology divergence barely predicts" foil.

**Post-Jan-2026 papers flagged:** GNNMTE (WWW 2026, ~Apr 2026); MUG (Feb 2026); GraphBFF (Feb 2026); DisRFM (Feb 2026); GPH² (2602.13075, Feb 2026); architecture-generalization GFM (Mar 2026); Riemannian gluing (Mar 2026); S2Aligner (May 2026); shared-latent-geometry (May 30, 2026). Caveat: GNNMTE full text was inaccessible (ACM paywall, no arXiv preprint found) — its baseline list and exact score construction should be re-verified once the PDF is obtainable.

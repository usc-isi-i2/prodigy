# Lit sweep 4/5 — Features vs topology in GNNs/graph SSL; ablation methodology; LP eval pitfalls

*Deep literature agent report, 2026-07-25. Post-Jan-2026 arXiv swept explicitly. Verbatim.*

## Closest papers (ranked by threat/relevance)

1. **GSTBench: A Benchmark Study on the Transferability of Graph Self-Supervised Learning** — arXiv 2509.06975 (Sep 2025). Standardized cross-dataset transfer benchmark (pretrain on ogbn-papers100M, 5 SSL methods); most graph SSL fails to transfer, some below random init; GraphMAE (feature reconstruction) transfers best. RELATION: adjacent-but-open + must-cite — same question ("what transfers in graph SSL?") but purely observational; no feature/topology surgery, no divergence correlate.
2. **Architecture Matters: Uncovering Implicit Mechanisms in Graph Contrastive Learning** — NeurIPS 2023 — arXiv 2311.02687. GCL gains come largely from the GNN architecture (graph convolution implicitly minimizes a neighbor-induced feature-alignment loss; normalization modules can replace negatives), not the contrastive loss. RELATION: must-cite — closest existing mechanistic account that "the graph operator does feature mixing"; also frames your BN result.
3. **Analyzing Data-Centric Properties for Graph Contrastive Learning** — NeurIPS 2022 — arXiv 2208.02810. Generic graph augmentations give only marginal gains over naive untrained baselines on standard benchmarks. RELATION: must-cite — establishes that graph-SSL benchmark wins are largely not from the graph-specific machinery.
4. **When Structure Doesn't Help: LLMs Do Not Read Text-Attributed Graphs as Effectively as We Expected** — LoG 2025 — arXiv 2511.16767 (Nov 2025, revised Apr 2026). Text-only LLM input already strong on TAG tasks; structural encodings marginal or negative; an MLP head on node embeddings matches GNN adapters. RELATION: adjacent-but-open — your feature-dominance conclusion, but for LLM readers, not frozen SSL GNN encoders; must-cite to differentiate.
5. **When Do Graph Foundation Models Transfer? A Data-Centric Theory** — ICML 2026 — arXiv 2605.29828 — **POST-CUTOFF: May 2026**. Graphon-based theory: cross-domain output shift decomposes into finite-sample terms + an intrinsic structural domain discrepancy. RELATION: contradicts (in emphasis) — puts structural mismatch at the center of GFM transfer, opposite of your ρ≈−0.9 feature-divergence result; you must engage it.
6. **Feature Distribution on Graph Topology Mediates the Effect of Graph Convolution (Homophily Perspective)** — ICML 2024 — arXiv 2402.04621. Formalizes feature-shuffle interventions and A–X (topology–feature) dependence; counterintuitively, within-class shuffle can improve GNNs. RELATION: methodological anchor — the recognized semantics of shuffle-type ablations; note their shuffle is class-conditional, yours is global, and their direction of effect differs.
7. **A Fair Evaluation of Graph Foundation Models for Node Property Prediction** — arXiv 2606.24509 — **POST-CUTOFF: June 2026**. Re-evaluates 9 GFMs under a unified protocol vs well-tuned GNNs; only PFN-style models win, at higher cost. RELATION: methodological anchor — evaluation-hygiene precedent for deflationary GFM claims.
8. **Graph Contrastive Learning versus Untrained Baselines: The Role of Dataset Size** — arXiv 2509.01541 (Sep 2025). Untrained GNNs, MLPs, and handcrafted statistics rival GCL on standard datasets; GCL wins only at scale. RELATION: must-cite — reviewers will demand an untrained-encoder control because of this line.
9. **When Graph Structure Becomes a Liability: Re-Evaluation of GNNs for Bitcoin Fraud under Temporal Shift** — arXiv 2604.19514 — **POST-CUTOFF: Apr 2026**. Random-forest-on-features beats all GNNs OOD (F1 .821 vs .689); randomly wired graphs outperform the real graph; prior wins were leakage. RELATION: supports your thesis (supervised setting); shows random-rewiring controls are becoming standard.
10. **GLNN: Graph-less Neural Networks** — ICLR 2022 — arXiv 2110.08727; **Correct & Smooth** — ICLR 2021 — arXiv 2010.13993; **NAFS** — ICML 2022 — arXiv 2206.08583; **SGC** — ICML 2019 — arXiv 1902.07153. Distilled MLPs match GNNs; graph-free MLP + label propagation beats GNNs; training-free feature smoothing rivals trained GNN/SSL embeddings; GCN ≈ K-step feature propagation + logistic regression. RELATION: must-cite block — the supervised "features + smoothing carry it" canon your claim generalizes to SSL transfer.
11. **HeaRT: Evaluating GNNs for Link Prediction: Current Pitfalls** — NeurIPS 2023 D&B — arXiv 2306.10453; **Implicit degree bias in the link prediction task** — arXiv 2405.14985; **Pitfalls in LP with GNNs (target-link inclusion)** — arXiv 2306.00899. Easy negatives inflate LP results; hard/personalized negatives collapse heuristics and reorder rankings; degree bias makes LP look easy; target-link leakage. RELATION: methodological anchors for your repaired evaluator.
12. **Does GNN Pretraining Help Molecular Representation?** — NeurIPS 2022 — arXiv 2207.06010. Ablations show SSL graph pretraining gains often statistically negligible; input features among the decisive factors. RELATION: must-cite — precedent for deflationary ablation of graph pretraining.
13. **GNNs Use Graphs When They Shouldn't** — ICML 2024 — arXiv 2309.04332. GNNs overfit structure even when ignoring it is optimal. RELATION: adjacent — the mirror-image failure mode; sharpens your framing that NM lands on the feature shortcut instead.
14. **Bridging Input Feature Spaces Towards Graph Foundation Models (ALL-IN)** — arXiv 2605.04834 — **POST-CUTOFF: May 2026**; **Evaluating Progress in GFMs** — arXiv 2603.10033 — **POST-CUTOFF: Feb/Mar 2026**; **LLM as Graph Kernel** — arXiv 2603.14937 — **POST-CUTOFF: Mar 2026**; **Text-space GFMs benchmark** — arXiv 2406.10727. The GFM community increasingly treats the input feature space as the transfer bottleneck (and 2603.10033 explicitly disentangles semantic vs representational shift). ## Answers to the six questions

**1. Feature-driven GNN performance (supervised).** Well established: GLNN (2110.08727), C&S (2010.13993), SGC (1902.07153), NAFS (2206.08583), "When Do We Need GNNs?" (2210.16979), homophily/node-distinguishability theory (2304.14274, 2407.09618), and Errica et al.'s structure-agnostic baselines (1912.09893). For **SSL specifically**, no one has shown "topology alone ≈ chance, features carry it" interventionally on a pretrained transferable encoder. The closest are observational/benchmark results: 2208.02810 (augmentations ≈ untrained baselines), 2311.02687 (architecture, not loss), 2509.01541 (untrained ≈ GCL), GSTBench 2509.06975 (most SSL doesn't transfer; feature-reconstruction objective transfers best), 2207.06010 (pretraining gains negligible in molecules). Your gap is real.

**2. TAGs + LLM embeddings.** Feature dominance is emerging but not settled as a mechanistic claim: 2511.16767 (structure marginal/negative for LLM readers, Nov 2025), GraphICL 2501.15755 (prompted general LLMs beat specialized graph-LLMs OOD), pure-LLM benchmark 2502.18771, CS-TAG (NeurIPS 2023 D&B) and text-space GFM benchmark 2406.10727 (text embeddings as the unifying space). None does zero/noise/shuffle surgery on a frozen SSL GNN over TAGs; your finding is consistent with but not subsumed by this literature.

**3. Ablation-methodology precedent.** The shuffle-vs-noise distinction (feature CONTENT vs feature–structure COUPLING) is recognized in exactly one strong precedent: 2402.04621 (ICML 2024) formalizes A–X dependence and class-conditional shuffle — but for supervised node classification, and finds shuffle can HELP. Degree-preserving vs random rewiring appears in fraud/fake-news GNN critique papers (2604.19514 — Apr 2026; 2512.06638) and in probing work (2303.03951 "Probing Graph Representations", 2408.03877 GraphProbe, 2206.08005 molecular SSL probing). A full factorial features×topology surgery on a PRETRAINED transfer encoder has no precedent I could find; no "permutation ablations lie" paper exists for graphs — you can own that methodological point (and should cite 2402.04621 to define shuffle semantics).

**4. LP evaluation.** HeaRT (2306.10453) shows easy negatives inflate results and heuristics/simple methods reorder under hard negatives; 2405.14985 shows degree bias makes LP benchmarks intrinsically easy and proposes degree-corrected evaluation; 2306.00899 covers target-link leakage; 2411.03845 (GAE reconsidered) is a useful supplement. I found **no prior demonstration that SSL graph embeddings beat CN/AA/Jaccard floors under degree-matched negatives out-of-domain** — your +0.11 AUC on a never-seen graph appears to be a first, provided you adopt HeaRT-style hard negatives too (reviewers will ask for both).

**5. "Feature-mixing operator" vs structural information per se.** The conceptual split exists: SGC (propagation = low-pass feature smoothing), NAFS (training-free smoothing suffices), 2311.02687 (graph convolution = implicit neighbor-feature alignment in GCL), positional-vs-structural theory (Srinivasan & Ribeiro, 1910.00452; labeling trick 2010.16103), structural/positional encoder work (GPSE 2307.07107, universal structural encoder 2504.10917). What's missing everywhere: an interventional test on a pretrained transferable encoder showing the graph is used ONLY as a feature-gathering device. That is your claim (b)+(a) combination.

**6. Scoop check (2025–2026).** No paper found stating "graph foundation models mostly transfer feature-space knowledge, not structure" as a mechanistic claim. Convergent pressure: GSTBench (Sep 2025), fair GFM evaluation 2606.24509 (**June 2026**), GFM benchmark 2603.10033 (**Feb/Mar 2026**), ALL-IN 2605.04834 (**May 2026**, feature-space as the bottleneck), Bitcoin liability 2604.19514 (**Apr 2026**), "Can GFMs Generalize Over Architecture?" 2603.22984 (**Mar 2026**), WWW 2026 "Node Feature Transfer Invariant Modeling". The theory paper 2605.29828 (**May 2026**, ICML 2026) pushes the OPPOSITE emphasis (structural discrepancy governs transfer) — cite and reconcile: their graphon regime assumes informative topology; your TAG regime empirically violates it.

## Verdict

**Novel, with a closing window.** The mechanistic package — factorial zero/noise/shuffle × intact-edges surgery on a frozen NM-pretrained in-context encoder, shuffle ≈ no-op vs noise ≈ chance dissociation, cross-graph transfer tracking feature divergence (ρ≈−0.9) not topology divergence, plus above-heuristic relational signal under a repaired LP evaluator — has no direct precedent. Every ingredient exists separately (supervised feature-dominance canon; GCL-architecture mechanisms; SSL-transfer benchmarks; LP pitfalls), so the contribution must be sold as interventional mechanism for SSL transfer, not as "features matter" (which reviewers will call known since GLNN/SGC).

Controls reviewers will demand:
1. **nhop>1 replication** of the feature-forcing experiments — your 1-hop setup makes topology use nearly impossible by construction; this is the single most attackable point.
2. **Untrained-encoder baseline** with identical features (2509.01541, 2311.02687): show trained NM > random-init, else "it's just architecture".
3. **SGC/NAFS/feature-propagation floor**: if K-step untrained smoothing matches NM transfer, your claim strengthens to "graph = feature gatherer"; if NM > SGC, quantify the residual.
4. **Degree-preserving (configuration-model) rewiring** alongside random rewiring — the edge-side analogue of shuffle vs noise (precedent: 2604.19514, 2402.04621).
5. **Class/label-conditional shuffle** in addition to global shuffle, and explicit engagement with 2402.04621's opposite-sign shuffle effect.
6. **Structural featurization retrain** (degree, LapPE/RWSE inputs) to show topology is not merely unreadable from bio-embedding inputs.
7. **LP eval**: HeaRT-style personalized hard negatives + degree-corrected negatives (2306.10453, 2405.14985), target-link leakage audit (2306.00899).
8. **Homophily/feature-label alignment statistics** per graph (2304.14274) to preempt "your Twitter graphs are trivially feature-homophilous".
9. **Scale sensitivity** (2509.01541: SSL advantages emerge with data).

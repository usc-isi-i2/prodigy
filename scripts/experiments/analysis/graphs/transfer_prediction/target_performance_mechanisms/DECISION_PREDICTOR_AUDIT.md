## Decision

Reject the proposed held-out-source value-sign predictor as the next experiment.
The independent reviewer retracted that experiment recommendation after reading
the existing failed selectors and geometry contrasts. A desirable result is not
by itself a grounded experimental hypothesis. Keep the publication ambition
active, but do not manufacture novelty by relabeling an old validation procedure.

## What the audit rules out

- `FINDINGS_SUPPORT_WEIGHTING.md`: balancing query classes preserves the
  opposite support-CV/query-loss association signs in all six comparisons per
  source. A weighting-only explanation of that reversal is insufficient.
- `FINDINGS_READOUT_SELECTION.md`: support-only CV improves panel means but
  misses the best fixed rule within targets, including bot detection. It is
  not evidence for a reliable context-utility predictor.
- `FINDINGS_MESSAGE_SCALE_DECISION.md`: dispersion has explicit cross-source
  counterexamples; sensitivity has opposite utility signs across targets.
  Previously inspected political geometry cannot become an untouched test set.
- `FINDINGS_SUPPORT_PROTOTYPE_CONTRAST.md`: the same fixed prototype improves
  after suppression at 2.5k and deteriorates at 50k, despite native ranking
  improvements in both. Uniformly better support geometry is contradicted.
- `FINDINGS_EARLY_SUPPORT_CONTRIBUTIONS.md`: the value change is distributed;
  ranking supports by transported contribution norm does not supply a sparse
  explanation of the collective gain.
- `FINDINGS_REFERENCE_TRANSPORT.md`: the fixed-routing affine map is verified,
  but its exactness is not an explanation of task utility. Query-label alignment
  of the resulting normalized contrast would largely re-express the outcome.

The closest concrete proposed statistic was leave-one-support-out,
class-balanced correct-class margin change under fixed-attention value
replacement. It avoids query-label leakage, but is another support-validation
score with an unestablished support-to-query generalization assumption. The
previous failures do not mathematically falsify this exact variant; they do
remove the rationale for launching it merely to fill a reviewer-requested box.

## Correct the explanation, not just the caveats

`FINDINGS_INITIALIZATION_PREDICTION.md` establishes that restoring the actual
training table preserves the early political suppression benefit: +7.2483
versus +7.2555 within-episode AUC points. Under restoration, accuracy rises
from .731120 to .808268. The 50k interface dependence therefore cannot explain
away the early effect. Conversely, the early result cannot erase the 50k
dependence or turn that checkpoint into an unqualified longer-trained replication.

`FINDINGS_CLASS_REFERENCE_ACCOUNTING.md` also establishes that evaluated social
label inputs are class-keyed Gaussian vectors passed through an untrained
projection, not semantic target-label embeddings. The private manuscript source
now states this explicitly and reports the early restoration result adjacent
to its political result. The existing PDF has not been rebuilt in this audit;
it therefore predates these source corrections.

## Contribution boundary and next decision

The established contribution is a consequential distinction between changing
support routing and changing the class-reference content it transports, with
opposing native effects in bot transfer and beneficial value replacement in
political transfer. The origin of content utility remains unexplained. Neither
another endpoint replication nor a retrospective geometric correlation resolves
that gap. An 8+ intellectual contribution is not established by this audit.

Before any further experiment, require a hypothesis that specifies which
task-relevant information graph context changes, why the trained computation
uses that information differently in the contrasted settings, and an intervention
distinguishable from the closed selector, prototype, initialization, directed-role,
and training-isolation branches. Do not impose universal sign prediction as a
mandatory paper format. Retain all failed tests and the current diagnostic draft;
do not expand the manuscript to substitute volume for the missing explanation.

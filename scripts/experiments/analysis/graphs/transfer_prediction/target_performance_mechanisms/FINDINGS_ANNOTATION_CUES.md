# Support-side repair is not confined to explicit-cue queries

**Result.** On covid-political queries whose entire sampled subgraph contains
none of the published annotation hashtags or their detected cleaned-text forms,
support-edge suppression improves hongkong AUC by **8.04-15.79 points** across
all three seeds and both streams. The equal-account sensitivity also improves
in all six comparisons, by 6.96-15.57 points. This rules out a repair confined
to queries carrying these explicit cues. It does **not** establish independently
annotated political ground truth or a cue-free support episode.

## Provenance and design

All 78,672 graph rows match uniquely to `full_user_data.csv` by all eight fields
of the released `user_data.csv`, including profile, label and six metadata
fields. The original table has no duplicate complete-row keys. This recovers
the pre-cleaning `raw_profile` without fuzzy text matching or assuming that
the two tables share row order. Every graph-row label and all 64 cached batches'
feature identities and labels are checked. All cleaned profiles lack `#`.

The fixed vocabulary is the 29 hashtags in
[Retweet-BERT Table 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.08349v4), visually inspected
before outcome-stratified scoring. We use membership only, not the table's
political polarity assignments. Two detectors are retained:

- **Original hashtag:** case-insensitive exact hashtag in the recovered raw bio.
- **Original or cleaned surface:** that detector OR a whole-token match in the
  actual cleaned bio; the same published words' digit-deleted forms are included
  because digits can be absent after upstream cleaning. No outcome-selected
  extensions, stemming, negation interpretation or new political labels.

Detector, query/support strata and the seven reused topology conditions were
fixed in the runtime before scoring. Every original/fresh stream, six source/seed
models, all declared strata and the raw prototype are retained. The audit makes
zero new model forwards and no training updates. Saved logits reproduce all
84 reused whole-stream metric cells exactly. These are new stratifications of
the existing experiment, not new independent replications.

## What is present in the data

15,832/78,672 graph rows (20.12%) carry a published hashtag in their raw profile.
The broader detector flags 23,771 (30.22%). Presence differs by dataset label:
8,033/59,276 class-0 rows versus 7,799/19,396 class-1 rows for original hashtags;
11,435 versus 12,336 for the broader detector. This is descriptive overlap,
not an estimate of how many labels were generated by each upstream heuristic.

Each stream has 3,072 query occurrences in 128 episodes. The stricter broader
detector retains 1,048/1,059 occurrences (965/962 distinct query accounts) with
no cue anywhere in the query subgraph. These contain only 98/75 class-1
occurrences, versus 950/984 class-0 occurrences. Class imbalance and repeated
accounts matter; neither a thousand occurrences nor six source/stream cells
should be presented as that many independent replications.

## Matched support-removal effects

AUC-point ranges over three seeds x two streams, support removal minus intact
inference, always leaving the query unchanged:

| Cue detector / absent region | Query occurrences, original / second | Hongkong | Ukraine |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original hashtag / query center | 2,448 / 2,466 | +4.84 to +11.32 | -0.36 to -0.07 |
| Original hashtag / query subgraph | 1,191 / 1,209 | +8.37 to +15.90 | +0.18 to +0.79 |
| Broader detector / query center | 2,175 / 2,146 | +6.05 to +11.37 | -0.59 to -0.16 |
| Broader detector / query subgraph | 1,048 / 1,059 | +8.04 to +15.79 | +0.35 to +1.36 |

For the broader query-subgraph-absent stratum, hongkong mean AUC changes from
.5644 to .6688; Ukraine changes from .8001 to .8097. Hongkong NLL improves in
all six cases, by .0774-.1962 nats; Ukraine NLL worsens in all six, despite its
small AUC gain. Suppression is a partial ranking repair, not recovery to the
stronger source or to the best available prediction rule.

Degree-preserving support rewiring remains much smaller: across all eighteen
hongkong seed/stream/draw cells in that stratum, AUC changes range from -0.0634
to +0.0366 points. The matched query partition is held fixed between conditions.
The larger removal benefit is not produced by choosing an advantageous rewire.

Raw prototypes decrease from .8228/.8489 on the complete streams to .6866/.7231
on the broader query-subgraph-absent strata. This shows that the explicit-cue
partition identifies harder examples for the text rule too. Different strata
have different populations; this comparison is **not the causal effect of
deleting words**, and AUCs across strata do not linearly decompose full AUC.

For the account-balanced sensitivity, each account's global class-1 probability
is averaged over its repeated occurrences within the stratum before scoring
AUC. The six positive hongkong effects survive this alternative weighting.
This addresses occurrence multiplicity, not support/episode dependence or
sampling uncertainty; reported ranges remain descriptive, not confidence intervals.

## What the test cannot settle

Every one of the 256 episodes contains at least seven support subgraphs with
original-hashtag cues (at least eight under the broader detector). No complete
cue-absent support set exists in these inputs. We therefore report the
all-input-absent strata as empty, with undefined metrics, not as a successful
control. Cues can still affect the supports and thus the label vectors even
when absent from the selected query and its neighborhood.

The source documentation also uses news-media endorsements, including retweets,
to construct labels. Row-level label-route flags and complete original
endorsement decisions were not recovered in this audit. A no-hashtag row must
not be relabeled "news-only" or "independently annotated." Other semantic
political cues, collection bias and graph/annotation overlap remain possible.

**Paper implication:** add a bounded result that support-side repair extends to
harder queries without the published textual cues. Retain the annotation caveat.
Do not claim the political dataset has been decontaminated or that this test
identifies the pretraining property responsible for the source gap.

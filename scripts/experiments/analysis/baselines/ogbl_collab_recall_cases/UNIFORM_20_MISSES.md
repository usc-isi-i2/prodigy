# Twenty uniformly sampled validation misses

I sampled **20 without replacement from all 18,797 misses** of the retained fresh-negative joint model (seed 0, update 150) on 2018 validation. This is the first draw, in draw order, using NumPy PCG64 seed **20260914**. No filtering for interesting cases or for agreement across seeds.

Here, an incorrect example means a **true collaboration that fails Hits@50**. The model does not supply a fixed binary classification threshold, so I did not combine these with a separately defined set of false positives. No 2019 data were opened.

The cutoff is **0.615730**. “Negatives ahead” counts negative scores greater than or equal to the positive's score; a hit requires fewer than 50. Similarity is cosine similarity of supplied author features, not a verified subject-area label. Node numbers are dataset IDs. Activity refers to graph-derived event incidences in 2017, not verified paper counts. Length-three signal refers to the cached feature; zero is not a claim that every conceivable longer path is absent. For nonzero-AA pairs that feature is not used as a general path census.

What this sample changes in the diagnosis:

- **Many misses have weak observed local support.** All 20 are novel pairs, 16 have no common collaborator, 10 have an endpoint with at most five historical collaborators, and 16 have at least one endpoint inactive in 2017. These groups overlap. “Previously active” in the warm-only experiment meant activity at any prior time; it did not mean active in 2017.
- **Some misses are demonstrably recoverable with existing information.** Frozen AA-DC recovers cases 4, 11, 17 and 18. Other optimization seeds recover case 7. This establishes complementary predictions, not a combination that improves total Hits@50; previous fusion and warm-only tests lost other hits.
- **Cutoff improvements address only part of the sampled problem.** Cases 7, 8, 17 and 18 have 58–70 negatives ahead; eight other cases have more than 1,000. Raising their scores or lowering the cutoff must still be assessed across the entire panel.

My interpretation: the earlier illustrative examples overemphasized useful structural predictions being discarded. That problem is real, but this uniform sample also contains many novel pairs with little direct graph support. A method that closes the gap must produce net additional hits—through better use of existing evidence, additional discriminating information, or both. These 20 cases do not establish which intervention will work, and observed attributes are not causal explanations of the model's decisions.

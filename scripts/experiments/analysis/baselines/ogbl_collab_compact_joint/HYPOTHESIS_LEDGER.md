# ogbl-collab hypothesis ledger

Closeout: 2026-09-14. This ledger reconstructs the sub-million-parameter
HyperFusion challenge as a sequence of hypotheses, tests, results, and explanations.
It is an index over the detailed findings rather than a replacement for their
protocols and validation receipts.

The target was to exceed HyperFusion's reported **71.29% test Hits@50** with fewer
than one million learned or fitted inference scalars. No leaderboard-acceptable win
was established. The strongest defensible forward result was **69.7979 +/- 0.1786%**
with **496,448** inference scalars; the best individual fresh-negative model reached
**70.1569%**.

“Because” below is deliberately conservative. It records a mechanism only when the
experiment isolated or directly diagnosed it. Otherwise it states the narrowest
interpretation supported by the evidence, not a post-hoc causal story.

## Research trajectory

| 5 | A compact learned joint scorer can beat AA by combining structural and pair features. | A roughly 496k-scalar joint scorer with handcrafted structural/pair inputs, BCE, hard negatives, and rolling-year selection. | The original model averaged 63.68% on 2018 validation, below AA-DC. | It recovered roughly 2.9k AA misses per seed but lost roughly 4.9k–5.6k AA hits. | 7 | A concrete score pathology, rather than the whole model, explains some fusion damage. | Identify and zero the negative self-pair while leaving every non-self score unchanged. | Best validation fusion improved from 67.6814% to 67.8250%. | The self-pair inflated the negative cutoff, so removing it rescued positives. | 8 | HyperFusion's released global mixing rule may extract more from the compact experts. | Replay the released construction with native and percentile scores; also optimize a post-hoc rank sum. | 63.47%, 66.21%, and about 69.16% mean test Hits@50, all below 71.29%. | The released rule uses whole score-vector similarities, not pair-specific correctness. | 9 | Expert complementarity may be realizable with a pair-conditional selector. | A five-fold, test-supervised shallow tree over expert scores, disagreements, and pair features. | 84.9864% cross-fitted test Hits@50 under the size cap. | The same test panel supplied labels for training folds. | 13 | Different fresh-model seeds make complementary errors, so a two-model average may cross the target while staying under 1M scalars. | Evaluate every equal-logit seed pair on 2018, with a validation-selected standalone control and a frozen advancement gate. | Best pair was 68.5607% versus 68.7155% for the control. | Averaging slightly improved broad ordering but worsened the top-50 tail; all pairs lost to the best standalone checkpoint. | Stop equal-logit seed ensembling; no 2019 evaluation. | 15 | Renewing the negative pool throughout training will turn better late generalization into a better selected model. | Replace the 100k negative pool every 50 updates, paired against rerun BCE controls. | Mean selected 2018 score was 68.8164%, 0.0743 points below control; only one seed improved. | Renewal substantially repaired late negative-panel overfit, but the best early checkpoint did not improve. | 16 | Optimizing the actual extreme tail will improve Hits@50 beyond BCE. | Add a fixed top-50 pairwise softplus loss to BCE. | Mean selected 2018 score was 68.2467%, 0.6441 points below control. | The loss flattened late degradation and improved the independent probe, but its rescues were outweighed by lost existing hits in two seeds. | 18 | Restricting training to warm endpoint pairs will better match the evaluation population. | Train the same compact joint model using only positives whose endpoints were previously active. | Mean selected 2018 Hits@50 fell from 68.8908% to 68.5124%; every seed and novel-pair slice regressed. | Population matching shifts which AA hits are recovered or lost but does not improve their net balance. | Reject warm-only positive filtering. | 19 | A small AA residual gate can rescue hard cases while retaining the structural baseline. | Rolling-year residual gate, followed by warm-endpoint, constrained-rescue, and direct-score variants. | The original temporally matched gate gained 0.7423 points over its frozen AA base but only 0.2263 over official-calibrated AA; later variants stayed around 66.6–67.9% and did not create a route to 71.29%. | Even unchanged positive scores can lose hits when corrected negatives raise the global cutoff. ## What the sequence established

The campaign ruled out a broad class of easy explanations. The gap is not explained
by an absent graph signal, one pathological self-pair, insufficient generic decoder
capacity, a single global fusion weight, ordinary seed averaging, fixed-negative
late overfit alone, the tested top-50 loss, warm-only population filtering, a shallow
SAGE encoder, or the tested compact NCN completion.

The recurring mechanism is a **recovery/retention conflict at a global extreme-tail
metric**. Learned models repeatedly recover AA-DC misses, especially novel or zero-AA
pairs, but also promote enough difficult negatives to raise the 50th-negative cutoff
and displace more established AA hits. Several interventions improve AUC, independent
negative probes, late-training behavior, or particular strata without improving the
selected full-panel Hits@50.

The remaining positive evidence is narrower:

1. Fresh-negative rolling refit reaches 69.80% defensibly and 70.16% for its best
   individual seed.
2. Pair features have ample within-year label-fitting capacity, but the learned rule
   does not transfer across years.
3. Temporal path age is directionally interesting but did not satisfy its support
   prerequisite.
4. A compressed published-style ranking backbone remains untested, not validated.

Because the 2019 panel was repeatedly inspected during development, a future local
score above 71.29% would still require full disclosure and would not by itself erase
the post-hoc selection history. The project-level success criterion remains external
leaderboard acceptance or another benchmark-maintainer-approved fresh evaluation.
